1. What Loyly does

Loyly is a personal sauna log. With Loyly you can:

  • time a sauna session round by round;
  • log each round's duration, temperature and cool-down method (cold plunge, cold shower, air, snow or none);
  • read a temperature from a thermometer photo or type it by hand;
  • rate how relaxed you felt and how ready for sleep you were, add a written note and record a short voice note;
  • see your sessions in a weekly timeline and a calendar, and see your own numbers in Trends;
  • create session cards and monthly reports, and manage the places where you go to the sauna.

2. Not medical advice

If you have a heart condition, high or low blood pressure, are pregnant, or have any other health concern, talk to a doctor before using saunas. Leave the sauna right away if you feel dizzy, unwell or overheated. You are responsible for your own safety.

3. Starting and ending a session
