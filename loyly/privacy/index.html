Loyly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Loyly app ("Loyly", "the app", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Loyly iOS app (bundle ID com.loyly.barbul).

Loyly is a personal sauna log. You record sessions, rounds, temperatures, cool-down methods and how a session felt, and the app shows your own numbers back to you.

If you have questions, contact us at LoylyComm@gmail.com.

1. Information you create in the app

When you use Loyly, the app saves the following so you can see it later:

  • Start information. When you tap Start my first session, the app creates a random identifier and a random passcode for this installation. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Preferences. Temperature unit (°C or °F), your usual place, the days and time you usually go to the sauna, and whether the screen stays awake during a round.
  • Sessions and rounds. Start and end times, the place you picked, and for each round: duration, temperature (optional), cool-down method and an optional short note.
  • How it felt. Optional relaxation and sleep ratings from 1 to 5 and an optional written note. These are your own ratings. Loyly does not interpret them and does not give health advice.
  • Places. Names and types of places you add, such as "Home" or "Gym".
  • Thermometer photos (optional). Photos you take or pick to read a thermometer, attached to a round.
  • Voice notes (optional). Short recordings (up to 30 seconds) you make on a session.
  • Embers. Your ember balance, ember spending (clean session cards, monthly reports, extra place slots) and the transaction identifiers needed to credit purchases once.

This information is saved in Loyly's app storage on your iPhone. We do not receive copies of your sessions, notes, photos or recordings. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you have it turned on) may include app data according to your iOS settings.

2. Permissions

Loyly asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change any permission later in iOS Settings.

Permission When it is requested What it is used for
Camera When you tap to scan a thermometer while logging a round Taking a photo of the sauna thermometer so the app can read the temperature
Photo Library (read) When you pick a thermometer photo from your library Reading the temperature from a photo you choose
Photo Library (add) When you save a session card Saving the session card image to your Photos
Microphone When you record a voice note on a session Recording a short note about how the session felt
Notifications The first time you reach the Today screen Loyly sends notifications related to the features you use in the app, according to how the app works. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings

If you decline a permission, the rest of the app keeps working. For example, you can always type a temperature by hand.

3. Reading thermometer temperatures

When you scan or pick a thermometer photo, Loyly uses Apple's Vision text recognition on your iPhone to find the number shown on the thermometer. The photo is not sent to us or to any third party for this. Only the photo you choose to keep is attached to that round.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Loyly does not collect facial data. It does not create face geometry, faceprints, face templates or any other biometric identifiers, and it does not use Face ID data. Thermometer photos are meant to show a thermometer, but a photo you take or pick might happen to include a person's face.

4.2 How such images are used

A photo is used only to read the temperature on the thermometer and to show the photo back to you on the round it belongs to. Loyly does not detect, analyze, recognize or measure faces in any photo.

4.3 Disclosure and sharing

We do not receive, disclose, share, sell or transfer these photos. They are kept in Loyly's app storage on your iPhone. A photo leaves the app only if you choose to share it yourself, for example through the iOS share sheet.

4.4 Retention

A photo is kept until you delete it. You can delete it from the round with the Delete control, by deleting the round or session, or with Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes it.

4.5 This section

This Section 4 is our complete explanation of how Loyly collects, uses, discloses, shares and retains any image that may include a face.

5. Third-party AI services

Loyly does not use third-party AI services. No session data, photos, recordings or text are sent to any AI provider.

6. In-app purchases

Embers are bought through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive or store your payment card details, Apple ID password or billing address. We receive only the transaction information StoreKit provides on your device, which the app uses to credit embers once.

7. Exports and sharing

You can save a session card to Photos or share a session card or monthly report through the iOS share sheet. Anything you share goes to the destination you pick and is then handled under that service's terms and privacy policy.

8. Analytics, advertising and tracking

Loyly does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not build an advertising profile about you.

9. Information we receive when you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to help you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose.

10. Legal pages

This Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use are web pages hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open them inside the app, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address under GitHub's own privacy statement.

11. Deleting your data

  • Delete a single item. Delete a photo, voice note, round, session or place from where it appears.
  • Delete All Data. Go to Settings → Delete All Data and confirm. This removes your sessions, rounds, notes, ratings, photos, voice notes, places you added, preferences, ember balance (including purchased embers), unlocked reports and extra place slots, and the start information created for this installation. The app then returns to the start screen. Built-in sample content, such as the Loyly Guide session, is part of the app and appears again.
  • End Session. Going to Settings → End Session returns you to the start screen without deleting your log.
  • Delete the app. Deleting Loyly from your iPhone removes its app storage.

12. Selling personal information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

13. Children

Loyly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Most information in Loyly is under your direct control in the app as described in Section 11. For anything else, email LoylyComm@gmail.com.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page before they take effect.
